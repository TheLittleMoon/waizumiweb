# WAIZUMI — Brand Brief

Source: Brand Discovery Questionnaire + live site (visa-waizumi.com) + logo.

## Identity
- **Name:** WAIZUMI Immigration Consulting Office
- **Japanese:** 行政書士わいずみともみ事務所
- **Principal:** Tomomi Waizumi / 和泉 知美
- **Since:** 2017 · Sapporo, Hokkaido
- **Audience:** ~half Hokkaido Japanese employers/families, ~half foreigners (late 20s–mid 40s)
- **Feel:** Trust · upright · quality · fairy-godmother care that “works magic”
- **Words:** Attention to detail, communication, genuine, cares about clients, quality

## Visual direction
- Logo accent: dusty rose / mauve `#886078`
- Tone: minimal, calm, feminine, polished, timeless, accessible (not luxury)
- Avoid: yellow, brown, red; black except for letterforms; loud/bubbly corporate purple gradients

## Languages
1. Build site structure & UX in **English**
2. Keep all original **Japanese** copy as source of truth for JP launch
3. Add **Chinese** later
